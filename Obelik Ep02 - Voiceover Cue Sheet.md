## Full script (continuous read, for one-take VO)

Is this… on record?

*(pause — title)*

For years, construction has run on scattered tools. Projects tracked through emails and WhatsApp messages. Budgets buried in spreadsheets. Payments made on trust, with no guarantee. And disputes that drag on in court. Five players, and not one shared system.

This is Obelik. A full-lifecycle construction and procurement platform — built for a global industry, with multi-currency support. From the first application, through bidding, construction, and final handover, every stage of a project lives in one place.

Obelik gives every role its own portal. A manager who funds the project. Contractors who build. Vendors who supply. A consultant who certifies the work. And an admin overseeing it all.

At the heart of Obelik is its escrow system. Money only moves when work is proven. First, the owner funds a secure escrow wallet, through an integrated financial-technology system. As work is completed, the contractor raises a claim for payment. The consultant — or architect — reviews the work and issues an interim payment certificate. Only then does the escrow release payment to the contractor, net of retention. From its own escrow, the contractor pays vendors for the supply of materials. And through a change order, the owner can pay a vendor directly. Every balance is live — straight from the ledger.

And it all begins with the people who hold the purse strings. In today's episode, we focus on the manager portal.
